# What does 2d6 mean?

2d6 means roll two six-sided dice and add their results. For example, rolls of 3 and 5 total 8. The possible total is 2 to 12.

## Reading the notation

The number before `d` tells you how many dice to roll. The number after it tells you how many sides each die has. Thus 1d8 uses one eight-sided die, while 3d4 uses three four-sided dice.

## Adding a modifier

For 2d6 + 2, add 2 after adding the dice. Rolls of 3 and 5 therefore give 10. A rule can instead ask you to subtract a modifier.

## When to roll

Roll when the game's rule or agreed procedure calls for that roll. The notation describes the dice; it does not decide whether the situation needs a roll, what the total means, or whether any other bonus applies. Check the rule that requested it.
